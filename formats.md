## Indoor Shooting — Wotton Range

At our indoor range in Wotton near Dorking we shoot .22 rifles:

- **Wednesdays 7:30pm** — standing and sitting position at 20 yards
- **Friday evenings** — prone (lying down) shooting

There are winter and summer leagues competing remotely with other clubs 
across Surrey. It's relaxed and friendly — a great place to start.

## Outdoor Shooting — Bisley

At Bisley ranges near Woking, typically twice a month, we shoot at 
distances from 100 to 1000 yards. Events include:

- Moving targets
- Timed targets
- Targets simulating hunting quarry
- Vintage rifle shooting including WW2 .303 rifles
- Modern scoped rifles

Bisley is one of the world's great shooting venues and offers a huge 
variety of formats and competitions to take part in.

## New Members

All abilities welcome. We have club equipment available and can guide 
you through everything you need to get started, including the licensing 
process. Get in touch via the home page to find out more.
