We are a friendly, welcoming rifle club with a wide variety of formats 
to shoot and a whole world of competitions to take part in.

Beginners are very welcome — we have club rifles and equipment to get 
you started, and can guide you through the licensing process.

We have our own indoor range in Wotton near Dorking for .22 smallbore 
shooting, and regularly use Bisley ranges near Woking for full bore 
shooting from 100 to 1000 yards.

Founded in 1900, safety has always been paramount. We are fully 
accredited and insured.

**Get in touch:** email info@leatherheadrifleclub.org.uk or call 07802 831073.
